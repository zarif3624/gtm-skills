# Revenue Enablement Leader Playbook

For revenue enablement leaders turning field evidence into approved learning, practice, and measurable behavior change. This playbook connects buyer and seller evidence without treating course completion, one call, or a revenue result as proof that enablement worked.

## Install

```bash
npx skills add zarif3624/gtm-skills --skill gtm-context --skill analyze-sales-call --skill analyze-win-loss --skill develop-positioning --skill prepare-demo --skill coach-sales-rep
```

Use only material approved for the intended learning audience. Remove credentials, unnecessary personal data, restricted account details, and content a facilitator or learner should not access. Confirm recording permissions, employee notice, customer permission, retention rules, and the authority to reuse any call, quote, logo, or account example before analysis or distribution.

## Day 1 - Define the business and learning decision

Start with the sales motion, buyer, approved claims, observed performance need, and decision the program should improve:

```text
Use $gtm-context to create or update our shared GTM context from approved product, buyer, process, and evidence sources. Separate Verified facts, Inferred interpretations, Hypotheses, and Unknowns. Identify source restrictions and the product, brand, legal, privacy, security, customer, and people-review owners required before reuse or rollout.
```

Write a behavior hypothesis, not a course request. State the observable seller decision or action, the buyer outcome it might support, the evidence behind the need, and what would disconfirm the hypothesis. Keep suggested scope, owner, audience, and rollout date **Proposed** or **Unknown** until the responsible owner accepts them.

## Day 2 - Build the field evidence base

Use a representative, approved sample rather than selecting only memorable calls:

```text
Use $analyze-sales-call on these approved transcripts. Extract buyer and seller evidence with locators, then identify observable choices that affected clarity, trust, or progress. Separate one episode from a tentative or repeated pattern. Do not score personality, infer private traits, or invent a universal benchmark.
```

Then test whether the call themes appear in broader decision evidence:

```text
Use $analyze-win-loss for [period, segment, and outcome population]. Keep buyer evidence, seller interpretation, CRM reason codes, and product or commercial records distinct. Report counts, sample size, source coverage, missingness, counterexamples, and alternative explanations beside each pattern.
```

One rep, one manager, one call, or one outcome does not establish a team-wide need. If the sample is small, biased, selectively recorded, or missing important groups, return an evidence-collection plan. Never publish customer content or internal findings outside the approved audience merely because analysis was permitted.

## Day 3 - Design the enablement asset around supportable claims

Turn the strongest field evidence into a message, decision guide, job aid, or practice scenario:

```text
Use $develop-positioning for [audience and buying situation] from the approved call and win/loss evidence. Build a claim ledger, alternative map, message pillars, proof status, limitations, objections, and one bounded message test. Keep product capability, expected workflow change, customer outcome, and public proof distinct.
```

The review matrix must account for product, customer proof, brand, legal, privacy, security, commercial, and regional approval. Customer evidence is not customer permission to use a name, quote, logo, recording, or result. Label the asset **Proposed** until accountable reviewers approve both its claims and intended audience.

## Day 4 - Turn the asset into observable practice

Build realistic practice around a buyer decision rather than recall alone:

```text
Use $prepare-demo to design a safe practice scenario for [buyer situation]. Use fictional or approved data, map each product moment to a supported buyer outcome, include questions and recovery paths, and surface capability, roadmap, security, privacy, and implementation claims that require authorized review.
```

Define the target behavior in terms another trained observer could identify. Give learners room to diagnose weak fit, surface a limitation, or accept a no. Passing practice does not authorize a product claim, customer disclosure, live-system change, or irreversible external action.

## Day 5 - Run a bounded coaching experiment

Use observed practice or approved live-call evidence for development:

```text
Use $coach-sales-rep with these approved excerpts, observations, and prior coaching notes. Separate behavior, interpretation, and outcome. Identify one or two behavior experiments, strengths to preserve, counterexamples, rep and manager responsibilities, and leading and disconfirming signals.
```

Ask for the rep's perspective before treating an interpretation as shared. Do not convert developmental evidence into ranking, surveillance, or a formal employment conclusion. Compensation, discipline, promotion, termination, quota, and other consequential people decisions stay with authorized managers and applicable HR, legal, privacy, and employee-relations reviewers.

## Measure the learning loop

**Training completion is not behavior change. Behavior change is not revenue impact.** Report these layers separately:

| Layer | Inspect | Do not claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Participation | Eligible audience, attendance, completion, practice attempts | Completion proves capability or adoption |
| Learning | Scenario decisions, artifact quality, learner explanation, confidence with context | A quiz or self-report proves live behavior |
| Behavior | Observable target behavior in an approved sample, counterexamples, manager and rep interpretation | One call proves a stable pattern |
| Buyer progress | Clarity, accepted next steps, disqualification quality, decision evidence | Seller behavior caused buyer action |
| Business outcome | Conversion, cycle, value, retention, or other approved metric with denominator and period | Association proves enablement caused revenue |

Record baseline, population, sample size, source coverage, observation window, exclusions, confounders, and missingness. Use `Hypothesis` when causality is untested. A credible result can be no change, a negative effect, or evidence that a different system constraint matters more.

## The operating cadence

- **Monthly:** review field evidence, source permissions, sample coverage, and contradictions.
- **Before building:** choose one consequential, learnable behavior and its disconfirming signal.
- **Before rollout:** complete content, claim, customer, privacy, security, accessibility, and people review as applicable.
- **During rollout:** collect participation and learning signals without presenting them as business impact.
- **After rollout:** compare behavior and buyer evidence with the baseline, preserve counterexamples, and decide whether to continue, revise, or stop.

Use the [evidence and status contract](../evidence-contract.md) when moving claims between call analysis, win/loss learning, positioning, practice, and coaching. Preserve source, audience, status, limitation, and approval so a polished enablement asset cannot silently turn a `Hypothesis` into a fact.
