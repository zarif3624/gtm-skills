# Forecast Methods

Choose based on available evidence.

## Deal Inspection

Best for a small number of material opportunities. Assess each deal using buyer progress, required milestones, qualification, timing, and contradictory evidence.

## Historical Conversion

Best for larger, repeatable motions with enough comparable history. Segment by meaningful differences such as market, source, product, deal size, or rep tenure. Avoid tiny cohorts.

## Stage Weighting

Use as a rough baseline only when stage definitions are consistent and stage-to-win rates are measured. Do not use arbitrary default probabilities as a final forecast.

## Cohort Or Velocity Model

Useful when pipeline creation, conversion, and cycle behavior are stable. Show sensitivity to each input.

## Scenario Structure

- **Downside:** closed revenue plus deals with strong verified progress, with elevated slip assumptions.
- **Expected:** the most supportable outcome under current evidence and measured behavior.
- **Upside:** plausible additional outcomes that require named milestones to occur on time.

Document the method, data window, segmentation, exclusions, and backtest result when one exists.
