# Strategic Account Planning Methods

## Three Planning Layers

- **Account:** hierarchy, priorities, current footprint, relationships, outcomes, and long-term investment.
- **Opportunity:** a specific buyer decision with qualification, competition, and next actions.
- **Customer delivery:** contracted commitments, adoption, support, renewal, and realized value.

Keep the layers connected without collapsing them. An active opportunity does not prove account-wide demand; customer adoption risk can change whether expansion is responsible.

## Whitespace Test

For each potential business unit and use case, ask:

1. Is the entity and need verified?
2. Does the current offer create relevant value?
3. Is there a supportable trigger or timing signal?
4. Is there a legitimate path to learn more?
5. Could delivery support the outcome?
6. What evidence would disconfirm the opportunity?

Use `Whitespace hypothesis` until buyer evidence establishes a real initiative. Do not calculate revenue potential from employee count or a generic penetration assumption.

## Relationship Coverage

Map business role, entity scope, current access, relationship owner, stance, actions taken, and evidence date. Avoid social closeness scores and personality labels. Multi-threading means understanding relevant roles with the current contact's context and an appropriate reason; it does not mean bypassing or escalating around them.

## Investment Ranking

Consider:

- expected customer value and strategic relevance;
- evidence strength and disconfirming signals;
- timing and decision access;
- delivery effort, obligations, and adoption risk;
- renewal or relationship impact;
- internal resource cost and learning value.

Use ranges and qualitative bands when data is incomplete. Do not hide material unknowns inside one account score.

## Portfolio Discipline

Separate:

- current contracted footprint;
- active validated opportunities;
- adoption or recovery work;
- renewal decisions;
- whitespace hypotheses;
- inactive or rejected ideas.

An account plan should choose where not to invest. Define review triggers and evidence that moves an item between states.
