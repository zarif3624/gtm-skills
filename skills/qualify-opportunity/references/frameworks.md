# Qualification Framework Map

Use the framework the team already understands. Do not mix labels unless the mapping helps expose a gap.

| Neutral dimension | MEDDPICC | SPICED | BANT |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Problem | Identify pain | Pain | Need |
| Impact and outcome | Metrics | Impact and critical event | Need and timeline |
| People | Champion, economic buyer | Decision stakeholders | Authority |
| Decision | Decision criteria, decision process | Decision | Authority |
| Commercial path | Paper process | Decision and critical event | Budget and timeline |
| Competition | Competition | Situation and decision | Usually implicit |

## Evidence Status

- **Confirmed:** direct, current evidence supports the item.
- **Partial:** some evidence exists, but an important element is missing.
- **Unknown:** no reliable evidence has been captured.
- **Contradicted:** available evidence conflicts with the qualification claim.

Do not calculate a percentage that hides a fatal contradiction. Surface hard risks separately, especially no problem ownership, no path to a decision, no viable implementation, or a buyer-requested pause.
