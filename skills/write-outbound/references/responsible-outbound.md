# Responsible Outbound Review

Before launching scaled outreach, have a responsible human verify:

- the lawful basis, consent, identification, and unsubscribe requirements that apply to each region and channel;
- the source and permitted use of contact data;
- suppression lists and opt-out propagation;
- sender identity, domains, and reply handling;
- message accuracy, proof, and personalization fallbacks;
- volume, frequency, quiet hours, and stop conditions;
- data retention and deletion requirements.

Channel notes:

- **Email:** use accurate sender and subject information; provide an operable opt-out where required.
- **Professional networks:** respect platform rules and avoid automated behavior that mimics a personal relationship.
- **Calls and SMS:** verify local calling, recording, consent, and time-of-day requirements before use.
- **AI assistance:** disclose automated interaction when the channel, policy, or context calls for it. Never let the agent fabricate a human action.

This checklist is operational guidance, not legal advice or a compliance determination.
