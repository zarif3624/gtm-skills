# Write Outbound

Write human, relevant outreach grounded in a real reason to contact the buyer. Do not manufacture familiarity or urgency.

## Before Writing

Read `.agents/gtm-context.md` and any account brief. Identify:

- recipient role and likely responsibility;
- verified account signal or honest segment-level reason;
- problem hypothesis and relevant value;
- available proof;
- channel, relationship, region, and sequence goal.

If personalization evidence is weak, use transparent role or segment relevance instead of pretending to know the person.

## Message Method

1. Open with the reason for the message.
2. Connect a verified signal or relevant role to one problem hypothesis.
3. State the value in the buyer's terms, without unsupported superlatives.
4. Add proof only when it is real and applicable.
5. Ask for one proportionate next step.
6. Make declining or opting out easy.

Keep one primary idea per message. Vary the reason to reply across a sequence; do not send the same email with different subject lines.

## Guardrails

- Never invent referrals, shared connections, customer names, quotes, results, intent signals, or observations.
- Never conceal identity or impersonate a human who did not send the message.
- Avoid false scarcity, fake deadlines, guilt, fear, or misleading `Re:` and `Fwd:` subjects.
- Do not claim compliance with outreach laws. Flag consent, identification, suppression, and opt-out requirements for human review.
- Do not scrape or use sensitive personal data for personalization.
- Stop a sequence after an opt-out, negative response, or other suppression signal.

Read [channel and review guidance](references/responsible-outbound.md) for higher-risk or scaled campaigns.

## Output

Provide:

1. audience and message hypothesis;
2. evidence used and missing;
3. sequence table with timing, channel, purpose, and copy;
4. subject-line options when email is used;
5. personalization fields with approved fallbacks;
6. stop conditions and human-review checks;
7. one test variable and success measure.

Do not optimize only for opens or replies. Prefer qualified conversations, positive replies, and low complaint rates.
