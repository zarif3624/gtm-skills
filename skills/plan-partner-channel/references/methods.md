# Partner And Channel Methods

## Choose The Motion By Job

| Partner type | Primary job | Common evidence of activation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Referral | Trusted introduction | Qualified buyer accepts an introduction |
| Reseller or distributor | Sell and transact | Registered opportunity meets qualification evidence |
| Services or implementation | Deliver customer outcomes | Approved joint scope enters delivery |
| Technology | Improve product workflow | Customer adopts the verified integration |
| Marketplace | Discovery or transaction route | Traceable buyer action through the marketplace |
| Co-sell | Coordinate relevant selling work | Buyer-accepted joint action with clear ownership |

Do not use the table as proof that a named partner can perform the job.

## Lifecycle Evidence

- **Target:** fits observable criteria; interest unknown.
- **Recruit:** mutual-value discussion is active.
- **Contracted:** authoritative agreement is executed.
- **Enabled:** required people completed approved readiness evidence.
- **Activated:** partner completed a real, relevant customer or market action.
- **Productive:** repeated customer outcomes or qualified contributions meet the approved standard.
- **Inactive:** no current evidence of the required motion or the relationship is paused.

Contracted, enabled, and activated are not interchangeable.

Assess each stage from its own evidence. Do not backfill `Target` or `Recruit` as complete merely because an agreement exists, and do not assume activation because enablement occurred.

## Economics

Model seller, partner, and customer effects. Include acquisition and enablement cost, margin or fee, delivery and support load, payment timing, returns or credits, overlap with direct selling, and renewal responsibility. Use ranges and scenarios when inputs are incomplete.

Never infer attractive economics from gross pipeline. Compare contribution margin, capacity use, outcome quality, cycle, retention, and conflict cost when the data supports it.

## Attribution Without Double Counting

Maintain one canonical opportunity and revenue record. Add contribution fields such as:

- `partner_sourced`: the partner created a traceable first qualified entry;
- `partner_influenced`: a documented partner action materially helped progress;
- `partner_delivered`: the partner owns approved implementation or service work;
- `partner_transacted`: the partner is the contractual transaction route.

One opportunity can have several contribution roles but only one canonical amount. Report role-specific views separately from total pipeline and revenue.

## Pilot Design

Start with a finite cohort whose fit and readiness can be inspected. Define required enablement, activation action, canonical tracking, customer permission, internal capacity, economic assumptions, and failure signals. Stop or redesign when partners cannot reach activation, customer outcomes suffer, conflict cost is high, or economics remain unsupported.
