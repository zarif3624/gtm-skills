# Negotiation Methods

## Interests Before Positions

A position is a requested term such as `20% discount` or `net 90`. An interest is the reason the term matters: budget timing, risk, cash flow, internal precedent, adoption uncertainty, or comparison with an alternative. Treat an unconfirmed interest as a hypothesis and ask before trading.

## Issue Map

For every material issue, record:

- current buyer and seller positions with sources;
- importance to each side;
- delivery or economic cost;
- flexibility and dependencies;
- who can discuss, recommend, approve, sign, and deliver;
- current status: `Unknown`, `Discussable`, `Proposed`, `Approved`, `Accepted by buyer`, or `Executed`.

Do not collapse `Approved` and `Accepted by buyer`; an internally approved option is not yet an agreement.

## Conditional Packages

Build packages around different ways to meet interests. Example structures, subject to approval:

- lower unit price in exchange for verified volume or longer commitment;
- payment timing traded against price or scope;
- narrower initial scope with expansion after agreed evidence;
- implementation sequencing traded against custom work or target date.

Use `If you can confirm X, we can seek approval for Y` when authority is not established. Never say `we will` for an unapproved term.

## Concession Ledger

Track each concession with its cost, buyer value, dependency, approval owner, status, and what was received in return. Avoid repeated one-way movement. A concession can be withheld because it is unapproved or unworkable; do not create a fake reason.

## Alternatives And Boundaries

State the best supportable alternative if no agreement is reached and the consequences of accepting a weak agreement. A walk-away point requires the appropriate owner's approval. Until then, label it `Proposed boundary`.

Pause when:

- the request exceeds known authority;
- a term creates unreviewed delivery, financial, legal, security, or privacy risk;
- the buyer introduces a material new condition;
- participants disagree on what was accepted;
- time pressure prevents accurate review.

## Meeting Discipline

Assign a lead, specialist roles, note-taker, and approval channel. Recap each issue as unresolved, proposed, internally approved, buyer-accepted, or executed. After the meeting, reconcile notes with the authoritative written agreement; meeting memory is not the contract.
