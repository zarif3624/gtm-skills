# Sales Call Analysis Rules

## Source Reliability

Use the strongest locator the source supports:

1. timestamp and speaker for a reliable transcript;
2. turn or line range for untimed text;
3. section heading for structured notes;
4. `Unlocated` when the source is a summary without traceable detail.

Do not create timestamps or quote marks for source material that does not contain them.

When speaker attribution is unclear, use `Speaker unknown` and avoid role-dependent conclusions. When the record is incomplete, name which conclusions may be missing or biased.

## Two-Layer Evidence Test

Keep these questions separate:

1. **Was it captured?** The source reliably shows that a named speaker made the statement or accepted the action.
2. **Is the claim true?** An authoritative source corroborates the content of the statement.

For example, a buyer saying "legal approved it" verifies that the buyer reported approval. It does not verify legal approval unless the authoritative record supports it.

Use:

- **Verified:** the specific statement, action, or external claim is directly supported by the cited source.
- **Inferred:** the interpretation is plausible but not directly established.
- **Unknown:** the source does not establish the item.
- **Contradicted:** reliable evidence conflicts with the item.

Write the scope of verification when ambiguity matters: `Verified statement; approval status Unknown`.

## Change Detection

Compare the conversation with prior state using:

- **New:** first reliable evidence of the item;
- **Confirmed:** current evidence supports an existing item;
- **Contradicted:** current evidence conflicts with it;
- **Unchanged:** discussed without material new evidence;
- **Unknown:** still unsupported.

Do not call repetition progress. Prefer new buyer actions, decisions, stakeholder access, completed validation, and accepted next steps.

## Coaching Boundaries

Good coaching describes a moment, behavior, evidence, likely effect, and alternative. Avoid labels such as weak, aggressive, disengaged, or strong communicator unless the behavior and decision standard are defined.

Do not infer emotion from tone, pauses, accents, disability, or speech patterns. Do not treat an automated sentiment score as evidence of intent. If recording, transcription, or analysis consent is uncertain, flag the applicable organizational and regional review without claiming compliance.
