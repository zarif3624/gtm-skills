## Positioning

For operations leaders coordinating repeated approvals, RelayFox is a workflow-approval product that routes approvals and records decision history. For teams relying on fragmented coordination, it may offer a more structured way to run an approval process. The audience problem and resulting value remain hypotheses until broader buyer research and workflow validation support them.

**Core message:** Route approvals through a defined workflow and retain the decision history.

**Language to hold:** faster approvals, less rework, savings, ROI, compliance, audit readiness, universal integrations, security outcomes, market leadership, customer trust, or guaranteed results.

## Bounded Message Test

Compare one primary framing—`defined approval routing` versus `recorded decision history`—with the same verified operations-leader audience, sender, channel, offer, timing, and review window. Measure accurate comprehension, qualified replies that confirm a relevant workflow, progression to a substantive conversation, opt-outs, complaints, and mistaken-role responses. A preference or reply can rank messages; it cannot verify the problem, product fit, or any held claim.
