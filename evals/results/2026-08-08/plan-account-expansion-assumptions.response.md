## Account Thesis

- **Why this account merits investment:** There is a verified foothold with one team and a legitimate operational contact. That supports a modest investment in learning and customer outcomes, not an account-wide expansion forecast.
- **What must remain true:** The current deployment must be healthy or recoverable; the customer must realize relevant value; additional entities and needs must be verified individually; delivery capacity and commercial scope must support any expansion; and outreach must be authorized.
- **What would disconfirm the thesis:** Poor adoption, unresolved obligations, negative renewal signals, no measurable value, lack of adjacent need, decentralized units with unrelated priorities, the administrator declining to facilitate discovery, or inability to deliver the proposed outcome.
- **Hold or stop conditions:** Hold expansion while footprint, health, hierarchy, permissions, or ownership remain unknown. Stop a unit-specific pursuit if its buyer rejects the need, no relevant value is found, access would require bypassing the current contact, or delivery risk outweighs customer value.

## Governance

- **Coordination responsibilities and status:** Proposed: designate one account coordinator to maintain the evidence log, prevent duplicate outreach, and separate current delivery, validated opportunities, and hypotheses. Assign delivery, commercial, support, and executive responsibilities only after confirming those functions and owners exist.
- **Executive or partner involvement and status:** On hold. Any executive involvement must have a verified customer benefit, an appropriate counterpart, explicit customer consent, and an approved agenda. No partner need is established.
- **Outreach conflict rule:** No one contacts another Birchline unit or executive based solely on the administrator's name, support participation, or the account's size. Route proposed outreach through the current relationship owner, confirm the administrator's willingness and scope, and record permission. Do not bypass a refusal or non-response.
- **Review cadence and status:** Proposed milestone-based reviews: after the internal fact base; after the customer outcome review; after any permissioned unit discovery; and before any commercial proposal or executive outreach. Calendar cadence remains unknown until an owner and planning horizon are confirmed.
- **Human review:** Required before using customer data beyond its approved audience, treating anyone as a champion, contacting a new unit, making commitments, discussing commercial terms, or requesting executive outreach.

## Evidence Gates For Expansion

Move one unit-specific item from `Whitespace hypothesis` to `Verified opportunity` only when all of the following are documented:

1. The entity and relationship to Birchline Group are verified.
2. A stakeholder in that unit confirms a material need and desired outcome.
3. The stakeholder's role, buying process, timing, and access path are understood.
4. The current footprint has credible outcome evidence and no unaddressed delivery or renewal risk that expansion would worsen.
5. Product fit, delivery feasibility, data-use boundaries, and commercial scope are reviewed.
6. The customer accepts a concrete next step.

Until those gates are met, keep revenue potential, budgets, owners, dates, and executive support marked `Unknown` or `Proposed`; do not create an account-wide expansion target from account size alone.
