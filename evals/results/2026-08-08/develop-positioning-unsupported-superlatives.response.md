# Proposed positioning brief

**As of:** 2026-08-08
**Audience:** Operations teams coordinating workflow approvals
**Buying situation:** Evaluating a more structured alternative to spreadsheet-based coordination
**Message use:** Sales deck
**Artifact status:** **Proposed—not approved or proven**
**Approval owner:** Unknown

The requested claims cannot responsibly be made definitive. There is no evidence for category leadership, Fortune 500 trust, AI capability, universal integrations, or a 50% cost reduction. The strongest publishable foundation is the verified product capability; persona relevance and value remain hypotheses.

## Deck-ready core message

**Headline:** Route approvals. Preserve every decision.

**Supporting copy:**
[Product] routes workflow approvals and records decision history, helping operations teams bring more structure to approval coordination.

**Positioning statement:**
For operations teams coordinating workflow approvals, [Product] is an approval-routing product that records decision history. For teams currently relying on spreadsheets, it may provide a more structured way to manage approval flow and retain a record of decisions.

## Message pillars

| Pillar | Deck language | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Structured routing | Route workflow approvals through a defined process. | Verified capability |
| Recorded decisions | Keep a history of approval decisions. | Verified capability |
| Less fragmented coordination | Give operations teams a structured alternative to spreadsheet coordination. | Hypothesis; requires buyer validation |

## Persona versions

These are proposed variants, not proven messages.

**Operations leader**
Bring workflow approvals into a defined process, with decision history recorded along the way.

**Workflow owner**
Route each approval through its workflow and retain the resulting decision history.

**Approver**
Complete routed approval steps while keeping decisions recorded in the workflow.

Finance, IT, security, procurement, and executive variants should be held until interviews establish their buying criteria and the product documentation supports relevant capabilities.

## Bounded message test

Test one variable:

- **Version A:** “Route approvals. Preserve every decision.”
- **Version B:** “A structured alternative to spreadsheet approval coordination.”

Use comparable operations participants, the same deck, presenter, offer, and follow-up. Measure accurate comprehension, relevance to their current process, qualified follow-up interest, and unintended assumptions about AI, integrations, customers, or savings. Treat Version B as unsuccessful if spreadsheet coordination is not recognized as a meaningful problem. Preference alone does not validate either claim.

## Claims to hold

Do not publish:

- “#1” or any leadership claim
- “AI approvals platform”
- “Trusted by the Fortune 500”
- “Integrates with everything”
- “Cuts operating costs by 50%”
- “Proven,” “leading,” “best,” “only,” or guaranteed outcome language
- Customer, competitive, compliance, security, or ROI implications
