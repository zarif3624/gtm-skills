## Executive Customer Summary

Pine Health purchased standard onboarding. The buyer also expects to launch within 30 days and understood from a sales conversation that a custom integration should be easy. Neither the integration nor the 30-day timeline has documented scope, approval, ownership, or an agreed delivery date.

The central handoff risk is an expectation gap, not a confirmed delivery commitment. The receiving team should acknowledge both goals, validate the integration promptly, and give Pine Health one written implementation plan that clearly confirms scope, owners, dependencies, acceptance criteria, and timing. Do not present the custom integration or 30-day launch as committed until that validation is complete.

## Implementation and Adoption

- **Prerequisites:** Confirm the standard-onboarding inclusions, completion criteria, launch definition, customer dependencies, resourcing, and access needs. None beyond the signed-order baseline are confirmed in the available context.
- **Integrations and data:** For the custom integration, capture the business use case, systems involved, data direction and fields, authentication method, volume, environment, testing approach, failure handling, support model, and acceptance criteria. Feasibility is currently unknown.
- **Security or legal follow-up:** Unknown. Determine during discovery whether the integration introduces security, privacy, data-processing, or legal review.
- **Training and change management:** Unknown. Confirm target users, administrator ownership, training needs, and launch communications at kickoff.
- **Key risks:** Unscoped integration work, an unvalidated 30-day target, no confirmed implementation owner, undefined launch criteria, unknown customer dependencies, and potential commercial change-control needs.
- **Risk response:** Prioritize expectation alignment and integration discovery before baselining the schedule. Maintain a written decision log so Pine Health receives clear answers without having to reconstruct prior conversations.

## Customer-Facing Kickoff Recap

Thank you for partnering with us on Pine Health’s launch. Our confirmed starting point is the standard onboarding described in the signed order. We have also captured your interest in a custom integration and your goal of launching within 30 days, so you will not need to restate that context from scratch.

At kickoff, we will align on the business outcome, what “launch” means, the minimum launch scope, and the dependencies on both teams. We will then assess the custom integration requirements and confirm in writing its feasibility, scope, ownership, timing, and any implications for the implementation plan.

The 30-day goal will remain a planning target while that validation is completed. Once the required scope, access, resources, and acceptance criteria are clear, we will provide a written plan with accountable owners and either confirm the target or explain the earliest supportable date and the options available to reach value sooner.

The immediate next step is to confirm the kickoff owner and attendees. We recommend including Pine Health’s business decision-maker and the technical or operational contact best placed to clarify the integration and launch requirements.
