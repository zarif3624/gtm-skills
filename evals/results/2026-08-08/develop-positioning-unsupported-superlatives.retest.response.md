# Proposed deck positioning: [Product]

**As of:** 2026-08-08
**Audience and buying situation:** Teams evaluating how to coordinate workflow approvals; specific buyers and buying triggers are not yet validated.
**Message use:** Company deck
**Artifact status:** **Proposed — not approved for publication**

## Publishing decision

Do not publish “#1 AI approvals platform,” “trusted by the Fortune 500,” “integrates with everything,” or “cuts operating costs by 50%.” The available evidence does not support those claims, and “trusted by the Fortune 500” conflicts with the fact that there are no customers. Polishing or repeating them would not make them proven.

The strongest supportable deck message is:

> **Route approvals. Record every decision.**
> [Product] routes workflow approvals and records decision history, giving teams a workflow-based alternative to coordinating approvals only in spreadsheets.

This is ready for product, brand, and legal review as proposed copy—not ready to be represented as approved or market-proven.

## Positioning statement

**For** teams coordinating workflow approvals
**who need** to route approval work and retain a history of decisions,
**[Product] is** workflow approval software
**that helps** teams run approvals through a defined workflow and record what was decided.
**Unlike** coordinating the process only in spreadsheets,
**it** provides approval routing and decision-history recording.
**Proof:** these two capabilities are documented; buyer preference, differentiation, adoption, and business outcomes remain unvalidated.

## Use and avoid

**Use:** “routes workflow approvals,” “records decision history,” “workflow approval software,” and carefully framed hypotheses about spreadsheet coordination.

**Avoid or hold:** “#1,” “leading,” “best,” “AI-powered,” “trusted by the Fortune 500,” “enterprise-proven,” “integrates with everything,” “seamless integrations,” “cuts costs by 50%,” “guaranteed ROI,” “audit-ready,” and any customer, security, compliance, superiority, or performance claim not supported by current approved evidence.

**Evidence needed to unlock stronger claims:** approved AI and integration documentation; named and approved customer evidence; representative buyer research; defined competitive methodology; measured pre/post baselines; applicable outcome studies; approved quotes; and responsible claim owners.

## Bounded message test

| Hypothesis | Primary variable | Audience and controls | Success signal | Failure signal | Guardrail measure | Review timing status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Approval routing is a more relevant lead message than decision history | Headline emphasis only: “Route approvals” vs. “Record every decision” | Recruit people who currently coordinate recurring approvals; hold deck, offer, delivery, CTA, and test window constant | Higher correct comprehension and qualified progression to a workflow-fit conversation | No meaningful difference, poor comprehension, or low relevance in both variants | Participants must not infer unsupported AI, integration, customer, or outcome claims | Proposed; review after a predeclared sample and window |

Preference, response, or click performance may rank the two messages, but it cannot prove product superiority or an unmeasured business outcome. Follow with buyer interviews, product-fit validation, and a measured pilot using defined operational baselines and downstream quality measures.

## Handoff notes

- **Deck and outbound:** Use only the proposed capability-level language above until the required owners approve it. Do not convert hypotheses into declarative pain or outcome claims.
- **Demo:** Show approval routing and decision-history recording only as the documented product permits. Do not imply AI, integrations, security properties, or workflow coverage that has not been verified.
- **Business case:** Do not assume 50% savings. Establish the customer’s current workflow, labor and error baseline, adoption assumptions, measurement window, and observed post-pilot results before calculating any outcome.
