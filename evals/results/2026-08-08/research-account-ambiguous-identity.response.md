The CRM record is not sufficient to identify the account: it contains only “Northstar Systems” and a contact role, with no domain, location, or legal entity. Do not personalize tomorrow’s call to either candidate until identity is confirmed. Open with a neutral identity check, then use the relevant branch of the discovery plan below.

## Tailored Discovery Questions

1. “Before we dive in, could you confirm your company website or headquarters and give me the one-sentence version of what Northstar Systems does?”
2. “What falls under your operations remit, and which outcomes are you accountable for this year?”
3. “What prompted this conversation now?”
4. “Walk me through the workflow that consumes the most attention today—from intake to completion.”
5. “Where do delays, rework, or exceptions usually enter that process?”
6. “What systems and teams touch it, and where are the manual handoffs?”
7. “How do you measure current performance, and what would have to improve for this to become a priority?”
8. “Who uses the process, who owns the technical environment, who approves change, and who could block implementation?”
9. **If Candidate A:** “How do remote monitoring, dispatch, order management, and customer systems exchange data today?”
10. **If Candidate B:** “Which security, quality, contracting, or customer-acceptance constraints must any operational change satisfy?”

2. If identity remains unresolved, use discovery question 1 as the opening, then select only the confirmed candidate branch; avoid references to warehouses, Canada, defense, customers, products, or technologies beforehand.
3. Immediately after the call, record the verified domain, location, legal entity, operating remit, current initiative, systems involved, success metric, stakeholders, and disqualifiers; then redo account research against that identity.
