**Whitespace validation**

13. Which other teams, if any, perform a comparable workflow or experience the same problem?
14. How do their needs, systems, governance, and success measures differ from the current team's?
15. Has any customer stakeholder asked to explore broader use? If so, what problem, value, timing, and decision process did they describe?
16. What evidence would show that broader deployment would not be useful or deliverable?

## Account Thesis

- **Why this account merits investment:** A live customer footprint and a support-engaged administrator provide a legitimate, bounded route to learn whether the current team is achieving value. The justified investment is in validation and customer outcomes, not yet in expansion selling.
- **What must remain true:** The product must support a measurable customer outcome; material commitments must be deliverable; current adoption and renewal health must be stable or recoverable; and any broader need must be stated or validated by an appropriate customer stakeholder.
- **What would disconfirm the thesis:** No meaningful current outcome, poor or declining usage without a recoverable path, unresolved serious obligations, non-renewal intent, lack of product fit, no comparable adjacent need, customer refusal of broader discovery, or delivery constraints that make expansion irresponsible.
- **Invest conditions:** Verified outcome or credible recovery plan, known renewal context, customer-approved discovery path, specific problem owner, and supportable delivery.
- **Hold conditions:** Unknown outcomes or health, unclear permission or authority, no customer-owned timing, or insufficient delivery capacity.
- **Stop conditions:** Confirmed lack of value or fit, explicit lack of interest, unacceptable delivery or relationship risk, or a rejected use case with no material new evidence.

## Investment Choices

1. **Invest now in customer truth:** contract, usage, support, outcomes, renewal, and obligations.
2. **Invest next in current-team value:** close adoption or delivery gaps before asking the customer to broaden use.
3. **Validate only one adjacent hypothesis:** select it based on a customer-described problem, not account size.
4. **Do not invest yet in account-wide campaigns, executive escalation, cross-division rollout, or revenue modeling.** Evidence and access are insufficient.
