## Open Decisions

- Does Meadow Labs want to use and co-own this plan?
- What business outcome is Meadow trying to achieve, and how will success be measured?
- Is there a buyer-driven reason to decide by the proposed target date?
- Who owns the decision and which stakeholders must participate?
- Which risk, security, privacy, legal, commercial, or procurement reviews—if any—are required?
- Who would own implementation, and what must be ready before signature?
- What review cadence, if any, does Meadow prefer?

## Dependencies and Risks

- The proposed target is not yet buyer-accepted and may be infeasible once Meadow's process and review durations are known.
- No milestone should be assigned a calendar deadline until Meadow confirms its process, owners, dependencies, and timing.
- Unknown success criteria could prevent a defensible decision.
- Unconfirmed reviewers or buying requirements could introduce delays.
- An unidentified implementation owner could create post-signature risk.
- If the target changes, Meadow and the seller should revisit the business reason, priority, dependencies, and feasibility rather than treating the change as a failure.

## Request to Meadow Labs

Please edit, accept, or reject this draft; identify the appropriate owners and required reviews; and confirm whether the proposed target is meaningful and feasible. Until then, all dates and milestones remain draft or proposed.
