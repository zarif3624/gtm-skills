# CrestBridge Referral Motion

**As of:** 2026-08-08
**Partner type and scope:** Referral; potential introductions to qualified target accounts
**Planning decision:** Do not add the claimed 100 accounts or $2 million to the company forecast, and do not send CrestBridge the customer list. Record the two figures only as unverified partner claims outside canonical pipeline. Validate the motion through a finite pilot before making any broader commitment.
**Program owner and status:** Owner `Unknown`; lifecycle status `Contracted` only

## Immediate decision

CrestBridge has a signed referral agreement, which proves only the `Contracted` stage. It does not prove account fit, access, buyer intent, enablement, activation, productivity, or forecastable revenue.

- **100 target accounts:** unverified partner claim. Do not create accounts, leads, or opportunities from the number alone. Request the proposed account names through an approved, minimum-necessary process, then check ICP fit, existing records, direct-seller ownership, duplicates, conflicts, and any applicable territory rules.
- **$2 million in pipeline:** unverified partner-entered estimate. Exclude it from forecast and canonical pipeline until each opportunity has a canonical ID, a validated account and buyer, documented buyer action, qualification evidence, an amount supported by the opportunity record, and an approved attribution status. Never create a second amount for an opportunity already owned by a direct seller.
- **Customer list:** do not share it. No customer consent, data-sharing approval, or authorized need-to-know scope is available. Expansion discovery should begin from approved, customer-specific referrals or another privacy-reviewed, minimum-data workflow—not a bulk customer export.

## Partner thesis

- **Customer outcome:** `Proposed` — customers receive a relevant, trusted introduction that improves discovery or expansion without creating channel conflict or unwanted data exposure.
- **Why a partner may improve it:** `Unknown` — CrestBridge may have relevant relationships, but no account-level access, buyer engagement, or capability evidence is available.
- **Target partner job:** Make qualified, buyer-accepted introductions for mutually approved accounts.
- **Evidence:** Executed referral agreement only.
- **Disconfirming signals:** Claimed accounts cannot be identified or matched to the ICP; overlap or ownership conflicts are material; buyers do not accept introductions; customer permissions cannot be obtained; qualification quality is poor; economics or internal capacity are unsupported.

## Operating model

1. **Account intake — Proposed:** CrestBridge submits a small pilot cohort through an approved channel, initially using only the minimum identifiers needed for matching. The company does not provide a customer list in return.
2. **Overlap and ownership review — Required before outreach:** an authorized owner, `Unknown`, matches submissions against canonical CRM records and checks direct-seller coverage, open opportunities, territory, prior contact, and customer restrictions. Existing seller ownership is preserved unless an approved conflict process decides otherwise.
3. **Registration — Proposed:** accept only a net-new or approved contribution against one canonical opportunity ID. Reject or merge duplicates. Registration does not itself establish forecastability or partner sourcing.
4. **Qualification — Required:** capture a real buyer/contact, problem, buyer-accepted next action, qualification evidence, estimated amount basis, and seller validation. Create or update the canonical opportunity only after the approved entry criteria are met.
5. **Customer permission — Required:** obtain documented permission before an introduction or before disclosing any customer-specific information. Share only the fields necessary for that approved action.
6. **Selling, contracting, delivery, support, renewal, and expansion:** responsibilities are `Unknown` beyond the signed referral agreement and must be confirmed by authorized owners. Do not promise delivery or support capacity.
7. **Disputes:** pause partner action on contested accounts while an authorized owner reviews canonical history, buyer preference, contribution evidence, and applicable agreement terms. Preserve customer choice.

## Pipeline attribution and deduplication

- Maintain one canonical opportunity ID and one canonical amount.
- Mark `partner_sourced` only when CrestBridge creates a traceable first qualified entry that meets the approved definition.
- Mark `partner_influenced` only when a documented CrestBridge action materially advances an existing opportunity.
- Mark `partner_delivered` or `partner_transacted` only if an approved role and evidence later support those fields; neither is currently established.
- Report role-specific contribution separately from total pipeline and revenue. Multiple contribution roles never create additional pipeline amounts.
- Existing direct ownership is separate from partner contribution. Resolve the possible overlap across the claimed accounts before assigning attribution.
- Forecast inclusion follows the company's approved opportunity-stage and buyer-evidence rules, not CrestBridge's label or estimate.

## Required human review

Before launch or any data exchange, authorized reviewers must confirm:

- the signed agreement's actual referral scope, economics, incentives, termination terms, and whether any proposed process is permitted;
- data, privacy, security, customer-consent, retention, and approved transfer controls;
- canonical CRM ownership, attribution, deduplication, territory, and conflict rules;
- brand, product, support, delivery, renewal, expansion, and customer commitments;
- competition, referral-fee, anti-bribery, tax, sanctions, and other applicable specialist questions.

Until those reviews and account-level evidence exist, CrestBridge remains `Contracted`, not `Activated` or `Productive`; its claimed accounts and pipeline remain unverified and outside the forecast, and no customer list is shared.
