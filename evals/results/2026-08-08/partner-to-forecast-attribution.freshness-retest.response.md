### Operating and attribution model

1. **Intake:** Keep the 100-account/$2 million submission in a non-forecast intake queue, not as 100 CRM opportunities. Require one row per proposed opportunity with account legal name/domain, buyer or buying group, customer consent and source evidence, problem/use case, amount and currency, expected timing, last meaningful buyer action, next buyer action/date, qualification evidence, and partner contact.
2. **Match and deduplicate:** Search by account/domain and any supplied source identifiers. If a direct opportunity exists, preserve its canonical ID, amount, and ownership; attach CrestBridge's documented contribution to that record. Never create a second revenue record to give the partner credit.
3. **Ownership:** Current ownership is `Unknown`. An authorized sales/CRM decision-maker must confirm ownership and conflict resolution. Contribution does not change ownership automatically, and customer route preference governs.
4. **Qualify and register:** Only after fit, buyer action, amount, timing, and stage evidence are reviewed may an authorized owner approve creation of exactly one canonical opportunity. Until then, classification is `Validate`, with $0 added to supportable pipeline.
5. **Attribution:**
   - `partner_sourced` only when CrestBridge created the traceable first qualified entry.
   - `partner_influenced` only for a documented CrestBridge action that materially advances an existing opportunity.
   - `partner_delivered` and `partner_transacted` remain false/unknown because the referral agreement does not establish those roles.
   - An opportunity may have multiple documented role flags, but its amount counts once in total pipeline and revenue. Partner-role views must remain separate from total pipeline.
6. **Information boundaries:** Share account, contact, pricing, product, security, or roadmap information only when the agreement, internal authority, and customer permission allow it. Data access, retention, deletion, dispute, renewal, support, and escalation rules are `Unknown` pending review.

### Economics and finite pilot

Downside, base, and upside economics are all `Unknown`: fee/rate, payment trigger and timing, margin, enablement cost, seller capacity, support load, overlap cost, conversion, cycle, retention, credits, tax, and customer effect are missing. The $2 million gross claim is not evidence of attractive economics.

**Proposed pilot:** After account-list permission and review, select a small finite cohort of non-overlapping, ICP-fit accounts. Enable the relevant CrestBridge people on the approved referral, consent, registration, and handoff process. Activation requires a buyer-accepted qualified introduction; success requires unique canonical tracking plus an approved standard for qualified contributions and customer outcome. Stop or redesign if permissions fail, duplicate/conflict rates are unacceptable, no activation is evidenced by the approved milestone-relative checkpoint, customer outcomes suffer, internal capacity is inadequate, or economics remain unsupported. Pilot size, thresholds, owner, and checkpoint are `Proposed/Unknown`, not commitments.

**Required human review:** agreement authority and scope; referral economics and incentives; tax, anti-bribery, sanctions, and competition issues; data, privacy, security, and customer consent; brand/product claims; delivery and support capacity; account conflict; attribution policy; and any customer commitment. Owners and approval status are `Unknown`.
