## Priority coaching experiment

**Target behavior:** Give buyers space to complete their thoughts and recover cleanly from accidental overlap.

**Replacement behavior:**

- Wait until the buyer finishes, pause briefly, then respond.
- Reflect or summarize the buyer’s last point before advancing.
- If overlap occurs, stop and say, “Please finish—I want to hear the rest.”

**Practice:** With the rep’s participation, review the two authorized excerpts and ask for their perspective, including whether audio delay or other context contributed. Rehearse three short buyer scenarios using the pause–reflect–respond sequence and provide a simple cue card.

**Live application:** Apply the behavior in the next three comparable buyer calls, subject to recording and access permissions.

**Progress signals:**

- The rep allows the buyer to finish in the reviewed moments.
- Any accidental overlap is acknowledged and repaired promptly.
- The rep can accurately summarize what the buyer said before responding.

**Failure or disconfirming signals:**

- Premature starts continue across the additional sample despite practice and support.
- Technical latency or another constraint explains the apparent interruptions.
- Turn-taking improves but buyer engagement or deal outcomes do not; this would weaken any claim that interruptions drove broader performance.

## Responsibilities

- **Rep — proposed:** Share their perspective, practice the replacement behavior, and identify support needed.
- **Manager — proposed:** Provide specific examples rather than trait labels, recognize improvements and counterexamples, and avoid treating deal outcome as proof of causation.
- **Enablement/system — unknown:** Supply an approved call-review standard, authorized recordings, and assistance with any audio-delay issue.

## Review trigger and evidence needed

Review after the next three comparable, authorized calls. Include timestamps, call context, buyer behavior, counterexamples, and the rep’s perspective. Before any formal action, also obtain the approved performance expectation, prior coaching history, support already provided, and applicable HR or employee-relations review.

**Replacement recommendation:** Do not recommend replacing the rep on this record. It supports a limited coaching experiment, not a conclusion about sustained performance or suitability for the role. Reconsideration would require a documented pattern across a representative sample, comparison with approved expectations, evidence that reasonable coaching and support were provided, the rep’s response, and authorized human review.
