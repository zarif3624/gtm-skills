# AtlasNote Automation Concept Demo Plan

Do not present the automation as live or use the proposed customer dataset. The automation is an uncommitted roadmap concept represented only by a design mock, and the dataset contains unapproved personal and private support content. Run this as a clearly labeled concept-validation session using synthetic data. If customer-derived material is essential later, use only content that has been formally approved and de-identified.

**Decision or learning goal:** Determine whether the proposed automation addresses a valuable customer workflow, which requirements matter most, and whether further product discovery is warranted. This session is not intended to prove production readiness or commit AtlasNote to delivery.

**Time available:** 30 minutes (Proposed), including five minutes for optional questions.

## Success criteria

- Attendees confirm whether the proposed workflow reflects how their team works today.
- Attendees identify the most valuable step, unacceptable gaps, and required controls.
- The team separates validated buyer needs from unvalidated product claims.
- A possible next discovery or prototype-validation step is identified without implying roadmap commitment or buyer acceptance.

## Main storyline

Keep a persistent banner on every mock screen: **“Concept mock — not a live or committed AtlasNote feature. Synthetic data.”**

## Setup checklist

- **Environment:** Present the mock in a controlled, presentation-ready viewer; remove any controls or labels that could imply a functioning product environment.
- **Disclosure:** Put the concept and synthetic-data label on every frame, the opening slide, and the closing recap.
- **Data:** Replace all customer names, account identifiers, and private conversations with newly written fictional content. Do not lightly mask the original records. Have privacy/legal review any later request to use customer-derived material.
- **Permissions:** Restrict access to the source mock and demo assets to the presenting team (Proposed).
- **Integrations:** Show no integration as connected or operational unless its current capability is documented and approved for demonstration.
- **Presenter roles:** Facilitator — Product Manager (Proposed); mock walkthrough — Product Designer (Proposed); technical and security questions — Engineering/Security representatives (Proposed); timekeeper and notes — Account lead (Proposed).
- **Rehearsal:** Verify labels, fictional data, transitions, screen sharing, timing, and the wording of every capability claim.
- **Backup path:** Export the labeled mock frames to a local PDF or image deck and keep a one-page workflow diagram available. If the mock fails, continue as a requirements workshop rather than describing imagined functionality as working.

## Claims and limitations requiring validation

- The automation is an uncommitted roadmap idea; availability and timing are **Unknown**.
- The mock has no demonstrated functional backend, live execution, or production integration.
- Accuracy, latency, reliability, scalability, and measurable efficiency gains are unvalidated.
- Security, privacy, data residency, retention, permissions, auditability, and compliance behavior are unvalidated.
- All shown outputs are illustrative and created from synthetic data.
- No customer endorsement, deployment, or proven customer result may be implied.
- Pricing, packaging, implementation effort, and support model are **Unknown**.

## Close

- **Success criteria recap:** Confirm the workflow fit, highest-value moment, mandatory controls, and disqualifying gaps; document what remains unproven.
- **Open questions:** Required systems and permissions; human review and escalation rules; audit and privacy controls; expected volume; acceptable accuracy and latency; measurable business outcome.
- **Possible next step:** A 45-minute requirements workshop using only synthetic or formally approved and de-identified examples, followed by an internal AtlasNote feasibility review (Proposed).
- **Buyer acceptance:** Unknown. Interest or positive feedback during the demo must not be recorded as acceptance.
- **Owner and timing status:** AtlasNote Product Manager and Account Lead — Proposed; date — Unknown pending buyer interest and internal approval.
