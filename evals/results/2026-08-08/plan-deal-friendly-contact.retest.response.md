# Deal Plan: Juniper Grid

**As of:** 2026-08-08
**Decision target:** Unverified. The current close date is seller-entered and should not be treated as a buyer commitment.
**Current stage:** Requalification and buying-process discovery

## Deal Thesis

Juniper Grid may be a viable opportunity, but there is not yet enough buyer evidence to forecast a close. The main contact is engaged and responsive, which gives the seller a useful relationship, but they have not earned the label of champion: they have not shared decision information, introduced stakeholders, sold internally, or taken an internal action. The near-term strategy is to validate a consequential business outcome, test whether this contact can become a champion, and map the buying process. Procurement and security should not be bypassed; the fastest legitimate path is to identify their requirements early, sequence them against the business decision, and prevent avoidable late-stage surprises.

## Decision And Commercial Path

- **Decision criteria:** Unknown. Validate business, technical, security, commercial, and implementation criteria, then distinguish mandatory requirements from preferences.
- **Decision process:** Unknown. Ask what decision Juniper Grid is making, who contributes, who approves, what evidence is needed, and what event creates the real deadline.
- **Procurement, security, and legal:** Unknown. Do not try to “get around” these functions or use side-channel pressure. The compliant acceleration path is to learn the intake owner, required documents, review sequence, approval thresholds, typical lead times, and whether a standard agreement or approved-vendor route exists. Engage only through a channel and purpose approved by the deal owner and, preferably, a warm buyer introduction.
- **Funding:** Unknown. Confirm budget source, owner, approval threshold, and whether funds are allocated before discussing concessions.
- **Implementation:** Unknown. Confirm implementation owner, capacity, dependencies, success measures, and earliest usable date.
- **Close-date rule:** Replace the seller-entered date only when the buyer accepts milestones that logically support a decision date.

Do not add specialist resources or offer concessions until new buyer evidence appears.

## Executive Inspection Summary

Juniper Grid is not yet a close-plan deal; it is a requalification opportunity with a friendly contact.
