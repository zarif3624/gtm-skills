# Qualification Assessment: ValeWorks

**As of:** 2026-08-08
**Framework:** Framework-neutral; no team-specific framework or exit criteria were provided
**Source material reviewed:** Opportunity context and notes supplied in the request

## Recommendation

**Continue discovery; do not classify this opportunity as Commit.**

The positive demo and verbal interest show engagement, but they do not establish a qualified buying decision. There is no reliable evidence of the business problem, impact, measurable outcome, economic buyer, decision process, funding, security path, implementation deadline, or a mutually accepted next step. The CRM probability of 90% and seller-selected Commit category are seller/CRM assertions, not buyer-validated evidence. Moving or retaining ValeWorks in Commit is therefore not justified.

- With no mutually accepted next step, there is no buyer-owned evidence that the deal is progressing.

## Stage Recommendation

- **Current stage and claimed exit evidence:** Proposal; $180,000 value; 90% CRM probability; seller forecast category Commit; positive demo and verbal interest. These are not sufficient Commit exit evidence.
- **Recommended stage:** Continue discovery and do not advance the pipeline stage. Retain Proposal only if local stage rules allow it while qualification is incomplete; otherwise return the opportunity to the last discovery stage. Change the forecast classification from Commit to the appropriate non-Commit category under the team’s rules.
- **Exit criteria still missing:** buyer-validated problem and impact; measurable outcome; identified economic buyer; confirmed funding; documented decision criteria and process; understood procurement and security path; feasible required implementation date; competitive/no-decision position; and a mutually accepted next step with owners and timing.
