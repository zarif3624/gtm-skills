### Canonical pipeline attribution

The following policy is `Proposed` and not yet approved:

- **Canonical opportunity ID and amount:** One CRM opportunity ID and one amount per buyer initiative. Amount, currency, stage, close date, and owner come from the canonical record, not the partner's aggregate report.
- **Partner sourced:** CrestBridge created the first traceable, qualified entry and the buyer accepted the introduction before an equivalent active opportunity existed.
- **Partner influenced:** A documented CrestBridge action materially advanced an existing opportunity; it does not create additional pipeline value.
- **Partner delivered:** Only when an approved scope gives CrestBridge responsibility for implementation or services; none is evidenced here.
- **Partner transacted:** Only when CrestBridge is the contractual transaction route; none is evidenced here.
- **Deduplication:** One opportunity may carry multiple role flags but its amount and revenue are counted once. Partner-role views must remain separate from total pipeline and revenue.
- **Disputes:** Freeze attribution and compensation treatment, preserve the canonical record, and route the evidence to an authorized reviewer.
