# Prospecting Plan: Regional Logistics Hypothesis Test

**As of:** 2026-08-08
**Objective:** Test whether independently sourced regional logistics firms consistently exhibit the problem, ability to realize value, and commercial outcomes needed to support a new-logo ICP.
**Pilot decision:** Do not build or activate a 1,000-account outreach list yet. Logistics is a hypothesis, not a proven best segment, and account fit is `Not scoreable`. First establish an approved, permission-safe account universe and run a bounded research and outreach pilot.

## Evidence And Scoreability Decision

- The evidence sample is three founder-referred customers: two regional logistics firms and one retailer.
- One logistics customer renewed; the other logistics customer and the retailer are too new to assess.
- This is not independent-sourcing evidence and does not include enough varied wins, losses, no-decisions, usage, value, retention, or delivery outcomes to calibrate account-level distinctions.
- There is no documented decision cost, observable scoring model, source coverage, missing-data policy, approved weighting rationale, calibrated thresholds, or authorized GTM owner.
- Therefore the model is **`Not scoreable`**. No account should receive a 0–100 score, provisional score, numeric ceiling, neutral score, or numeric tier cutoff. In the prospect list, `fit_score` must remain blank and `fit_status` must be `Not scoreable` until the scoreability gate passes.
- Calling logistics the “proven best segment” would overstate the evidence. The defensible claim is: **regional logistics is the leading segment hypothesis in a very small, founder-referred sample, with one observed renewal.**

## Target And Exclusions

- **Target segment:** Regional logistics firms, after “regional,” geography, company boundaries, and the relevant painful job are defined through observable fields and approved by a GTM owner.
- **Hard exclusions:** None approved. Do not invent or operationalize exclusions. Before outreach, an authorized GTM owner must approve hard exclusions, exception rules, and their evidence.
- **Caution signals:** Missing company identity or domain; uncertain segment classification; no independently verified problem signal; unverified trigger; unclear ability to realize value; unknown delivery burden; missing contact permission; unknown suppression status; or reliance on founder referral as the sole evidence.
- **Evidence limits:** No account universe, source-coverage assessment, market size, conversion history, buying-trigger evidence, approved disqualifiers, contact permissions, suppression data, or outreach authorization is available.

## Pilot And Quality Gates

- **Selection method:** Build a deduplicated research frame from approved sources. Select independently sourced logistics accounts across the observable attributes under test, plus an owner-approved comparator cohort. Do not cherry-pick lookalikes to the two referred customers.
- **Pilot size and rationale:** `Unknown` until the available universe, source coverage, permissions, motion cost, and owner are known. The authorized owner should approve the smallest batch that contains meaningful variation and can be reviewed account by account; the pilot is not a 1,000-account launch.
- **Deduplication:** Canonicalize domain and company identity; resolve parent/subsidiary treatment through an approved rule; merge duplicates before enrichment or contact.
- **Suppression and opt-out:** No activation without an authoritative suppression source, current opt-out check, applicable consent/privacy review, and approved outreach motion. Suppressed, opted-out, unverified, or permission-unknown contacts are blocked.
- **Minimum evidence before outreach:** Verified account and domain; approved segment definition match; observable problem evidence; any required value-realization evidence; approved exclusion check; relevant and verified contact role; source provenance and freshness; contact permission; clean suppression status; and outreach approval.
- **Stop or revise conditions:** Stop if permissions or suppression controls are unavailable; source coverage is materially unknown; the segment cannot be defined observably; required signals cannot be verified; independently sourced logistics firms do not repeatedly confirm the hypothesized problem; acquisition, activation, value, retention, or delivery evidence fails to distinguish logistics; or owner approval is absent.

## Validation And Scale Gates

1. **Research gate:** Approve the sales decision, false-positive/false-negative costs, segment definition, required observable criteria, disqualifiers, sources, missing-data handling, permissions, suppressions, and owner.
2. **Pilot outreach gate:** Approve the motion and pilot selection after every candidate passes the minimum-evidence checks.
3. **Learning gate:** Compare logistics with counterexamples or an approved comparator on independently sourced progression, activation, value, delivery burden, and—when enough time has elapsed—retention. Predeclare success and failure thresholds with the authorized owner; they are currently `Unknown` and must not be invented after results are seen.
4. **Scoreability gate:** Only consider 0–100 scoring after a sufficiently varied outcome history exists, proposed criteria demonstrate discrimination, missing-data behavior is documented, a holdout or prospective cohort validates the model, and the authorized GTM owner approves weights, thresholds, exceptions, and review timing.
5. **Scale gate:** Expand toward 1,000 accounts only if the pilot passes its preapproved success criteria, data and permission systems support the volume, and the owner approves scale. Scale in reviewed batches with suppression rechecks and learning reviews, not as one immediate blast.

## Handoff

- **Fields passed to account research:** Account identity, canonical domain, segment evidence, geography evidence, observable problem signals, value-realization signals, triggers, exclusion evidence, sources and locators, verification dates, missing fields, tier state, and notes.
- **Fields passed to outreach:** Only approved, verified records: account identity, qualitative tier, fit evidence, fit status, current trigger evidence, verified contact and buying role, reason to contact, personalization evidence, contact permission, suppression status, last verification time, owner, and approved motion metadata. `fit_score` remains blank.
- **Owner and review date:** Authorized GTM owner `Unknown`; approval status `Unknown`. Review is dependency-based: after the research frame and governance rules are approved, after each pilot batch, and before any scoring or scale decision.

## Weekly Learning Questions

1. Which observable problem and value-realization signals recur across independently sourced logistics accounts, and which are merely similarities to the referred customers?
2. Which criteria separate meaningful progression, activation, value, retention, and delivery outcomes from losses or no-decisions?
3. What false positives, false negatives, counterexamples, missing-data patterns, source gaps, permission failures, and suppression issues appeared?
4. Does logistics outperform an approved comparator on the predeclared outcomes, or is the “best segment” claim still unsupported?
5. What evidence and owner approvals remain before the next batch, a numeric model, or broader scale is allowed?
