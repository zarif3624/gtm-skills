# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Be respectful, specific, and generous with context. Welcome contributors across experience levels and sales disciplines. Critique ideas and artifacts, not people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate privacy violations, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope And Enforcement

This standard applies in repository discussions, pull requests, issues, and community spaces representing the project. Report conduct or privacy concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). The maintainer may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
